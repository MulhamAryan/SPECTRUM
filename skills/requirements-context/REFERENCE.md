## Evaluation

Minimum evaluation cases:

- complete_context_for_individual_requirement;
- missing_entity_definition;
- missing_abstraction_level;
- missing_source_or_parent;
- conflicting_source_evidence;
- missing_condition_of_use;
- missing_state_or_mode_context;
- missing_constraints_and_risk_basis;
- undefined_project_term;
- insufficient_stakeholder_context;
- context_sufficient_but_requirement_wording_defective;
- no_false_positive_from_generic_context_checklist.
